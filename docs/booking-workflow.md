## SECTION 1: QUALIFICATION QUESTIONS

Five questions. Natural, fast to fill out, just enough to prepare.

---

### Q1: Brokerage Name
**Field type:** Text (required)
**Label:** What's your brokerage called?
**Why it matters:** Charbel researches the brokerage before the call -- website, listings, social presence. The name is also pulled into every follow-up email for personalization.

---

### Q2: Team Size
**Field type:** Dropdown (required)
**Label:** How many agents are on your team?
**Options:**
- Just me (solo agent)
- 2-5 agents
- 6-15 agents
- 16-30 agents
- 30+ agents

**Why it matters:** Agent count maps directly to pricing tier. 1-5 = Starter lead ($149/mo). 6-15 = Professional target ($599/mo). 16+ = Enterprise conversation ($1,200/mo). Charbel knows which plan to lead with before the call starts.

---

### Q3: Monthly Lead Volume
**Field type:** Dropdown (required)
**Label:** How many inbound leads do you get per month, roughly?
**Options:**
- Under 50
- 50-150
- 150-500
- 500+
- Not sure

**Why it matters:** Volume determines the size of the problem. A brokerage generating 500+ leads and losing 30% to slow response has a six-figure revenue leak. One at 20 leads is a harder sell. This shapes the math Charbel runs on the call.

---

### Q4: Lead Channels
**Field type:** Multi-select checkbox (required)
**Label:** How do most of your leads reach you right now?
**Options:**
- WhatsApp
- Website form or contact page
- Phone calls
- Referrals only
- Paid ads (Zillow, Google, Facebook)
- Social media DMs

**Why it matters:** WhatsApp-first brokerages are the ideal fit -- Wakeeli plugs in directly. Web form answers tell Charbel there is a gap between inquiry and first response (prime pitch angle). Multi-channel answers show operational complexity Wakeeli simplifies.

---

### Q5: Attribution
**Field type:** Dropdown (optional)
**Label:** How did you hear about Wakeeli?
**Options:**
- LinkedIn (post or profile)
- Someone referred me
- Google search
- Cold outreach from Charbel
- Other

**Why it matters:** Attribution data for future outreach strategy. Also tells Charbel the warm-up level of the prospect and how to open the call naturally.

## SECTION 2: CALENDAR SETUP -- CAL.COM (FREE TIER)

**Recommended tool: Cal.com**
Free. No forced branding on the booking page. Better form customization than Calendly free. Zapier-compatible for future automation. Self-hostable if needed.

---

### Step 1: Account Setup
1. Sign up at cal.com with your business email (e.g. charbel@wakeeli.ai)
2. Set your timezone: **Eastern Time (ET)** -- US brokerage owners are spread across ET, CT, MT, PT; ET is the universal anchor
3. Upload a professional headshot -- not a logo. People book calls with people.
4. Display name: **Charbel Salameh, Founder @ Wakeeli**

---

### Step 2: Create the Event Type
1. Click "New Event Type" and select "One-on-One"
2. **Event name:** See Wakeeli In Action (25 Min)
3. **Duration:** 25 minutes (not 30 -- feels more respectful of their time, same substance)
4. **Location:** Paste your permanent Zoom or Google Meet link
5. **URL slug:** `/charbel/wakeeli-demo`

---

### Step 3: Availability Windows
**Recommended schedule:**
- Monday to Thursday: 9:00 AM to 12:00 PM ET and 2:00 PM to 5:00 PM ET
- Friday: Blocked (reserved for outreach, follow-ups, and admin)

**Reasoning:**
- Blocking 12 to 2 PM prevents back-to-back fatigue and forces a real break
- Avoiding Fridays protects one full day per week for non-call work
- Morning slots consistently show higher attendance rates for US professionals
- 6 slots per day maximum, capped at 3 calls (with buffer time -- see Step 4)

**Buffer time:** 15 minutes after each call. Non-negotiable. You need time to send the follow-up while the conversation is fresh, take notes, and reset before the next one.

**Max calls per day:** Set to 3 in Cal.com settings. More than that as a solo founder and your conversion quality drops.

---

### Step 4: Booking Page Description
This is what prospects see before they book. Outcome-led. No feature list.

> Your agents are probably responding to leads hours after they come in. By then, half of those people have already talked to someone else.
>
> Wakeeli is an AI system that responds to every WhatsApp lead within 60 seconds -- 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. It qualifies the lead, answers questions, and books property tours automatically. Your agents only step in when someone is ready to see a home.
>
> In this 25-minute call, I'll show you exactly how it works and what the numbers could look like for your brokerage. No deck. No pressure. Just a live demo and a straight conversation.
>
> Pick a time that works for you.

---

### Step 5: Add the Qualification Questions
In Cal.com, go to "Booking Questions" after creating the event:
1. Your brokerage name -- text, required
2. How many agents on your team? -- dropdown, required
3. Monthly inbound leads, roughly? -- dropdown, required
4. How do most leads reach you? -- multi-select, required
5. How did you hear about Wakeeli? -- dropdown, optional

---

### Step 6: Confirmation Page Message
The screen prospects see immediately after booking:

> You're booked. I'll see you on [Date] at [Time] ET.
>
> A confirmation email with the call link is on its way -- check your inbox (and spam, just in case).
>
> No prep needed. Come as you are. I'll do the heavy lifting.
>
> -- Charbel

---

### Step 7: Calendar Integration
Connect your Google Calendar or Outlook under Settings. Enable "Prevent Double Bookings." This ensures Cal.com respects existing events and never double-books you.

---

### Step 8: Reminder Automation (Free Setup)
**Email reminders:** Cal.com free tier includes automated email reminders. Set:
- 24-hour reminder: ON
- 1-hour reminder: ON (use the SMS instead -- see Section 3C -- but email backup is fine)

**SMS reminders:** Cal.com free tier does not include SMS. Options:
- Use a free Twilio number + Zapier free tier to trigger the 1-hour SMS
- Or send it manually -- it takes 30 seconds and the personal touch is worth it

## SECTION 4: THE FULL WORKFLOW MAP

Every touchpoint in order. What triggers what. What is automated vs. what Charbel does manually.

---

### PHASE 1: OUTREACH TO BOOKING

**Step 1 [Manual]** -- Charbel connects with brokerage owner on LinkedIn

**Step 2 [Manual]** -- Charbel sends a short, outcome-led message. No pitch. One line on what Wakeeli does and a question about whether they are open to a quick call.

**Step 3 [Manual]** -- Prospect replies with interest. Charbel sends the booking link: `cal.com/charbel/wakeeli-demo`

**Step 4 [Automated]** -- Prospect fills out the 5-question form and selects a time slot on Cal.com.

**Step 5 [Automated]** -- Confirmation email fires immediately (Cal.com).

**Step 6 [Manual, 10 min]** -- Charbel receives the booking notification. Reviews the form answers. Looks up the brokerage online -- website, listings, Google Maps reviews, social presence. Notes agent count and lead volume to shape the call.

---

### PHASE 2: PRE-CALL SEQUENCE

**Step 7 [Automated]** -- 24-hour reminder email fires (Cal.com).

**Step 8 [Automated or Manual]** -- 1-hour reminder SMS fires (Twilio + Zapier, or Charbel sends manually).

**Step 9 [Manual]** -- Charbel joins the Zoom/Meet link 2 minutes early.

---

### PHASE 3: THE 25-MINUTE CALL

**Step 10 [Manual]** -- Charbel runs the demo using this structure:

- **Min 0-2:** Open with their specific situation using form answers ("You mentioned most leads come in via WhatsApp -- how is your team handling that right now?")
- **Min 2-7:** Live demo of Wakeeli responding to a WhatsApp lead in real time
- **Min 7-17:** "What could this look like for [Brokerage Name]?" -- run the math together
- **Min 17-22:** Handle objections
- **Min 22-25:** Close -- "I'd like to run a free 30-day pilot with you. Can we start this week?"

**Step 11** -- Three possible outcomes:

- **YES:** Move directly to Phase 5 (pilot onboarding)
- **MAYBE / THINKING:** Move to Phase 4A (post-call follow-up)
- **HARD NO:** Thank them, close gracefully, note the account for a future re-engage in 90 days

---

### PHASE 4A: POST-CALL FOLLOW-UP (NOT YET COMMITTED)

**Step 12 [Manual]** -- Same-day follow-up email with the math (sent within 2 hours of the call -- Section 3G)

**Step 13 [Manual]** -- Day 3 follow-up with one new insight (Section 3H)

**Step 14 [Manual]** -- Day 7 final follow-up (Section 3I)

If no response after Day 7: mark as "re-engage in 60 days" and move on.

---

### PHASE 4B: NO-SHOW RECOVERY

**Step 12b [Manual or Automated]** -- 30-minute no-show email (Section 3D)

**Step 13b [Manual]** -- Day 2 follow-up with social proof stat (Section 3E)

**Step 14b [Manual]** -- Day 5 final re-engage or close loop (Section 3F)

If prospect rebooks: return to Phase 2. If no response: mark as "re-engage in 30 days."

---

### PHASE 5: PILOT ONBOARDING (YES SECURED)

**Step 15 [Manual]** -- Charbel sends onboarding document (PDF or Notion page) with next steps and what to expect in the 30 days.

**Step 16 [Manual + Shared Screen]** -- WhatsApp Business API connection setup, guided by Charbel. Typically 30-45 minutes.

**Step 17 [Manual, Weekly]** -- 30-day free pilot runs. Charbel monitors manually. Sends one weekly check-in message with any highlights (leads responded, tours booked).

**Step 18 [Manual]** -- Day 25 check-in call. Review actual results. Present paid plan options. Close the conversion to a paying client.
