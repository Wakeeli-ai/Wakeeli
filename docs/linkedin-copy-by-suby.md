## COPYWRITING NOTES (for Fox's reference)

**The Greased Chute Rule (Suby):** Every line exists to pull the reader into the next. Never let the pace drop. Cut every sentence that doesn't move them forward.

**The Larger Market Formula:** Most agencies don't know they have a response problem. They think they have a lead problem. Lead with that reframe. You're not selling software. You're diagnosing a problem they already feel but can't name.

**The Godfather Offer principle:** The CTA is never "buy." It's "let me show you the numbers on a 15-minute call." Zero risk. Maximum curiosity. Always frame the next step as value, not a sale.

**NEWIF in the About Section:**
- Need: agencies bleeding leads to silence
- Empathy: I sat with those transcripts myself, I saw it firsthand
- Want: more deals from leads you already have
- Irresistibility: AI that works 24/7, never misses, costs less than one lost deal
- Fear: your competitor is already moving on this

**Specificity is proof:** "31 out of 47 leads" is not a stat. It's a crime scene. Specificity creates credibility that round numbers never will. Always use the real numbers.
