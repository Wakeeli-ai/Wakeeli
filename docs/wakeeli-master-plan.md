### 1.3 The Demo Call (20 Minutes)

**Goal:** Qualify, show, close. Do not pitch. Let the demo do the work.

**Minute 0 to 5: Discovery**
Ask only these three questions:
1. "How many new leads are you getting per month right now?"
2. "What's your team's average response time when a new lead comes in?"
3. "What happens to the leads that don't get called back within an hour?"

Listen. Let them tell you the problem.

**Minute 5 to 15: Demo**
Screen share. Show a real lead coming in. Show the bot replying in under 60 seconds. Show it qualifying. Show it asking to book a tour. Show the calendar invite going out.

Say only this while it runs:
"This is what happens to every lead, from the second they fill out your form, at any hour, including 2am."

**Minute 15 to 20: The ask**
"Here's what I want to propose. You let me run this on your incoming leads for 30 days at no cost. I track every conversation, every tour booked, every conversion. At the end of 30 days, you have hard data. If the numbers are good, we talk about what makes sense going forward. If they're not, you've lost nothing.

I'm only doing this with 3 brokerages. I have 2 spots left. Do you want to be one of them?"

### 1.4 Objection Handling

**"We already have a CRM that does follow-up."**
"CRMs send one email and call it done. Wakeeli has a two-way conversation. It qualifies, handles objections, and books the tour. The CRM still gets the data. Wakeeli just handles the conversation that the CRM can't."

**"I don't have time to set this up."**
"I set everything up. You give me access to your lead source and 30 minutes to walk through your qualification criteria. That's the full time investment on your end."

**"We've tried chatbots before."**
"What you tried was a flow chart with canned responses. This is a Claude-powered AI that understands context, adapts to what the lead says, and knows when to hand off to a human. The output is tours booked, not chat transcripts."

**"What if it sounds robotic?"**
"Fair concern. That's exactly why I'm running pilots with live data before I scale. I'll show you real conversations from the test period. If the quality isn't there, we fix it before it goes live on your leads."

**"I need to think about it."**
"Understood. What specifically feels unclear? I'd rather answer it now than let you sit on a question that has a simple answer."

### 4.1 Monthly Client Health Check

Every client gets a monthly check-in. This is not optional. This is how you prevent churn before it starts.

**The monthly check-in (30 minutes):**
- Review the numbers: conversations, qualified leads, tours booked
- Identify one flow that can be improved this month
- Ask: "Is there anything you wish the AI was doing differently?"
- Preview next month: "We're testing a new re-engagement sequence for leads that went cold. Want to be in the beta?"

**Early churn signals:**
- Client stops responding to your Slack/WhatsApp messages
- Login activity drops (if you have analytics)
- Benchmark numbers are flat for two consecutive weeks

**Response to churn signals:** Do not wait. Call them. "I noticed [X metric] is flat this week. Can we jump on a 15-minute call to look at what's happening?"

## MINDSET AND PRIORITIES

*Framework: Alex Hormozi. One metric. One constraint. One focus.*

---

### The Only Metric That Matters Each Week (By Phase)

**Phase 0 (Week 1 to 2):** Is the product stable enough to demo? Yes or no.

**Phase 1 (Week 1 to 4):** Number of demo calls booked this week. Nothing else.

**Phase 2 (Week 4 to 8):** Are the pilot numbers good enough to publish as a case study? Yes or no.

**Phase 3 (Week 8 to 16):** MRR. That is the scoreboard.

**Phase 4 (Ongoing):** MRR minus churn. Net new revenue that sticks.

---

### What to Say No To

Until MRR hits $5,000, say no to:
- Conferences and events
- Partnership conversations that do not directly result in a new client
- Building features no paying client has asked for
- Social media platforms other than LinkedIn
- Inbound interest from verticals other than real estate (stay focused)
- Any tool, software, or subscription that is not directly tied to getting the next client

---

### How to Know If It Is Working

**Green lights:**
- Getting 3 to 5 demo calls per week
- Demo call show-up rate above 70%
- At least 1 person per month moves from demo to client
- LinkedIn content getting meaningful engagement from brokerage owners (comments, not just likes)

**Red lights:**
- Less than 1 demo call per week after Week 4
- Everyone on demo calls says "interesting but not ready"
- No response to follow-up messages from anyone

**What to do if you hit red lights:**
Do not pivot the product. Change the message first. Book 10 more calls and ask every single person: "What would have to be true for this to be an obvious yes for you?" Listen. The market will tell you what is wrong.

---

### Weekly Routine (Non-Negotiable)

**Monday:**
- Review last week's outreach numbers
- Set the week's single goal (1 demo call minimum)
- Write and schedule the week's LinkedIn posts

**Tuesday to Thursday:**
- Morning: 30 minutes on Sales Navigator outreach
- Afternoon: respond to DMs, prep for demo calls, work on product/flows

**Friday:**
- Demo calls (schedule them on Fridays so prospects are in planning mode)
- Review: what worked in outreach this week, what did not
- Update the case study doc with any new data from pilots

**Weekend:**
- Do not work. Seriously. Solo founders who burn out in Month 3 never make it to Month 6.
