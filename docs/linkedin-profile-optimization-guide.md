## 1. Profile Photo

**Goal:** Founder credibility at first glance.

- Headshot only. Shoulders and above.
- Plain background: white, light grey, or a soft navy that matches the Wakeeli brand.
- Direct eye contact with the lens. Not smiling at something off-screen.
- Natural light or softbox. No ring light halos. No harsh shadows.
- Dress the part: clean, sharp. Founder casual works (open collar, no tie). Not a passport photo. Not a party photo.
- No logos on the photo. No filters. No cropping from a group shot.
- File size: under 8MB. Square crop. LinkedIn will auto-circle it but shoot square.

**One test:** If you showed this photo to a Lebanese real estate agency owner in Dubai, would they take a meeting? If yes, use it.

## 2. Banner (1584 x 396px)

**Goal:** One message. No clutter.

**Specs:**
- 1584 x 396px, PNG or JPG
- Safe zone: keep key text and visuals in the center 60% (edges get cropped on mobile)

**What to put on it (pick one):**

Option A: Outcome statement
> Dark navy background. White headline text: "Lebanese real estate agencies close more deals with Wakeeli." Wakeeli logo bottom right corner. Clean.

Option B: Product in action
> Screenshot or mockup of the Wakeeli WhatsApp chat interface on a phone. One line of copy above it. Logo bottom right.

Option C: Social proof
> Agency logo + a one-line result. Example: "Agency X booked 3x more tours in 30 days." Keep it sparse.

**Rules:**
- Max two fonts. Max three colors.
- No stock photos of buildings, keys, or handshakes.
- No gradient rainbow or busy textures.
- The banner is a billboard seen for 2 seconds. One message wins.

**Tool:** Canva (free). Search "LinkedIn Banner" template. Strip it down.

## 3. Headline

**Limit:** 220 characters

**Rule:** Lead with the outcome you create, not your job title. "Founder at Wakeeli" tells no one anything. The headline is your pitch to strangers.

**Keywords to include:** AI, real estate, GCC, PropTech, lead conversion, WhatsApp automation

**Three structural options:**

**Option A: Outcome-first**
> Helping GCC real estate agencies convert more leads with AI | Founder at Wakeeli | PropTech | WhatsApp Automation

**Option B: Problem-solution**
> Real estate leads go cold in minutes. Wakeeli responds in seconds. | AI Lead Conversion for GCC Agencies | Founder

**Option C: Bold claim**
> I built the AI that books property tours on WhatsApp, automatically | Wakeeli | GCC PropTech | Real Estate AI

**Which to use:** Option A is the safest and most searchable. Option B is the most scroll-stopping. Test one for 30 days. Check profile views in analytics. Switch if needed.

**What not to do:**
- Do not write "CEO | Founder | Entrepreneur | Visionary." That is noise.
- Do not leave it as the LinkedIn default (your last job title).

## 4. About Section

**Limit:** 2600 characters

**Structure:** Hook, Problem, Origin Story, Solution, CTA

**How to write each part:**

**Hook (first 2 lines, visible before "see more"):**
The hook must work without clicking. Lead with the problem or a sharp statement.
> "Most real estate agencies in the GCC lose their best leads before a human ever picks up the phone."

**The Problem (2-3 short paragraphs):**
Describe what broken looks like for a Lebanese or GCC agency owner. Leads going cold. WhatsApp messages piling up. Agents chasing instead of closing. Keep it vivid and specific. No corporate language.

**The Origin Story (2-3 paragraphs):**
How did Wakeeli start? What did Fox see that others missed? A real moment, not a press release. Founders who sound human get trust faster than founders who sound polished.

**The Solution (1-2 paragraphs):**
What Wakeeli does in plain language. Not features. What it does for the agency. Specifically: it responds to inbound leads 24/7 on WhatsApp, qualifies them, matches them to listings, and books tours. Human agents only step in when the lead is warm.

**Numbers (sprinkle these in):**
- Forbes: 35-50% of sales go to the first responder
- 21x higher conversion when responding within 5 minutes
- Wakeeli responds in seconds, not hours

**CTA (last line):**
> "If you run a real estate agency and want to see how this works for your team, send me a message."

**Formatting rules:**
- Short paragraphs. Two to four sentences max each.
- One blank line between every paragraph.
- No bullet lists in the About section. They kill the narrative flow.
- No buzzwords: passionate, visionary, synergy, disrupting.
- Write in first person, present tense.

## 5. Featured Section

**Goal:** Pin your three best proof assets.

**How to add:** Profile > Featured > Add a post, link, or media file.

**Pin these three in order:**

1. **Origin story post**
   Your best long-form LinkedIn post telling why you built Wakeeli. If you have not written this yet, write it first. This is the post that converts profile visitors into followers and DMs.

2. **Demo video or case study**
   A screen recording of the Wakeeli product in action, or a written case study with real numbers from a pilot agency. Real beats polished here. A Loom recording with your voice over the product is fine.

3. **Lead magnet or demo CTA**
   A link to a demo booking page, a Wakeeli landing page, or a one-page PDF on "How AI Lead Conversion Works for Real Estate Agencies." Something a stranger can click and take the next step with.

**Rules:**
- Three items max. More dilutes the signal.
- Thumbnail matters. If LinkedIn pulls a bad default thumbnail, upload a custom one.
- Check that all links work every 30 days.

## 6. Experience Section

**How to fill in the Wakeeli entry:**

- **Title:** Founder and CEO
- **Company:** Wakeeli (create the LinkedIn company page first if not done)
- **Start date:** The month you started building, not the month you launched.
- **Location:** Beirut, Lebanon (or Lebanon / Remote if GCC-facing)
- **Description:** One sentence on what the company does, one sentence on who it serves.

Example description:
> Wakeeli is an AI-powered lead conversion platform for real estate agencies in the GCC. We connect to agencies' WhatsApp Business accounts and handle inbound leads automatically, from first response to tour booking. Currently working with agencies across Lebanon and expanding into the UAE and Saudi markets.

**What not to do:**
- Do not list features. That belongs on the website.
- Do not use passive voice. "Was responsible for building" is weak. "Built" is clean.
- Do not leave the description blank.

**Other experience entries:**
- List any prior roles that show business or tech credibility. Even if brief.
- One to two bullet points per old role. Focus on what you built or shipped.

## 8. Creator Mode

**Turn it on:** Profile > Resources > Creator mode > Turn on

**Why:** Creator mode moves your Follow button to the primary CTA (more followers, larger reach) and unlocks the content topics feature.

**Pick two content topics:**

1. Artificial Intelligence
2. Real Estate

These show up on your profile and signal to LinkedIn's algorithm what your content is about. Every post you make gets distributed more heavily to people who follow these topics.

**After turning on creator mode:**
- Your Featured section moves up on your profile.
- Your profile shows your recent posts directly.
- You get access to LinkedIn's newsletter feature when ready.

## 9. Connection Strategy

**Target profile:**
- Agency Owners, Managing Directors, Brokers, Sales Managers
- Location: UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Lebanon
- Industry: Real Estate, Property Development
- Company size: 2 to 50 employees (boutique and mid-size agencies)

**Daily volume:** 30 to 50 connection requests per day. LinkedIn soft-limits aggressively above this. Staying under 50 avoids account restrictions.

**Personalized note (always):**
LinkedIn gives you 300 characters. Use them. Blank requests get ignored.

Template to adapt:
> "Hey [Name], I'm Fox, founder of Wakeeli. We built an AI that handles WhatsApp leads automatically for real estate agencies in the GCC. Thought it was worth connecting. No pitch, just building the right network."

**Rules:**
- Personalize every note. Reference their company or location if you know it.
- Do not pitch in the connection request. Connect first. Build familiarity. Reach out later.
- After connecting, do not send an automated follow-up message immediately. That is how you get muted.
- Follow up 3 to 5 days later with a relevant post or a one-line question.

**Search approach:**
LinkedIn search > People > Filter by: Location (UAE, Saudi, Qatar) + Industry (Real Estate) + Title (Owner, Director, CEO, Broker)

## 10. Posting Setup

**Frequency for first 10 days:** 2 to 3 posts per day. Volume in the first 10 days trains the algorithm and shows LinkedIn your account is active. It is uncomfortable. Do it anyway.

**Best posting times (GCC / Beirut timezone):**
- Morning: 8:00 to 9:00 AM
- Midday: 12:00 to 1:00 PM

Post at the start of these windows, not the end.

**Three content pillars:**

**Pillar 1: Education**
Teach something your audience does not know. How AI lead conversion works. What happens to a lead in the first 5 minutes. Why most agencies lose deals at the first message. Pure value, no pitch.

**Pillar 2: Behind the scenes**
Show the build. A screenshot of the product. A decision you made and why. What it looks like to run a startup from Beirut targeting the GCC. People follow founders who show the work.

**Pillar 3: Proof**
Share results. Agency feedback (even early-stage). Stats. Screenshots from pilot conversations (blurred if needed). Specific numbers build credibility faster than any claim.

**Post format rules:**
- First line is the only thing visible in the feed. Make it hit hard. One punchy sentence.
- Short paragraphs. One to three lines each. One blank line between.
- End every post with one question or a direct statement. This triggers comments.
- No hashtag dumps. Two to three hashtags max, at the bottom.
- No stock photo attachments. Use real screenshots, real product, real moments.

**Scheduling tool:** LinkedIn's native scheduler works fine. Use it for morning posts if you are not awake at 8 AM.

## 11. First 30 Days Checklist

### Week 1: Foundation (Days 1-7)

**Day 1:**
- Upload new profile photo
- Design and upload banner
- Rewrite headline using one of the three options above

**Day 2:**
- Write and publish the About section
- Fill in Experience (Wakeeli entry complete with description)
- Add all 10 skills

**Day 3:**
- Turn on Creator Mode
- Set content topics (AI + Real Estate)
- Create Wakeeli company page on LinkedIn if not done

**Day 4:**
- Write your origin story post (why you built Wakeeli). Publish it.
- Pin it to Featured section.

**Day 5:**
- Start connection outreach. 30 to 50 requests, all personalized.
- Post: education pillar (one thing agency owners get wrong about lead response time).

**Day 6:**
- Continue connection outreach.
- Post: behind the scenes (show something from the build or the product).

**Day 7:**
- Review your profile as a stranger would. Fix anything that reads stiff or unclear.
- Post: proof or stats pillar.

### Week 2: Momentum (Days 8-14)

**Days 8-14 daily:**
- 30 to 50 connection requests per day, personalized
- 2 posts per day (one education, one behind the scenes or proof)
- Reply to every comment within 2 hours. Comments boost reach dramatically.
- Check LinkedIn analytics: profile views, post impressions. Note what performed.

**Day 10:**
- Add demo video or case study to Featured section (second pin)
- Add lead magnet or demo CTA link to Featured section (third pin)

### Week 3: Outreach (Days 15-21)

**Days 15-21:**
- Begin messaging newly connected agency owners. One to two sentences. Reference something specific about their agency. Ask one question. No pitch yet.
- Post 2x per day, continuing all three pillars
- Identify top 20 target accounts (specific agencies in UAE, Saudi, Qatar you want as clients). Follow them. Comment on their company posts.

**Day 18:**
- Write a longer-form post (700 to 1000 words) on how AI changes lead conversion for GCC agencies. This is your highest-value educational post. Publish at 8 AM.

### Week 4: Convert (Days 22-30)

**Days 22-30:**
- Shift from mass outreach to focused follow-up with warm connections
- Offer a no-pitch demo call: "10 minutes, I'll show you exactly how it works for your agency"
- Post 2x per day, lean heavier on proof and case study content
- Track which posts drive the most connection requests and DMs. Double down on that format.

**Day 30 review:**
- Profile views vs. Day 1 baseline
- Connection growth
- Post reach and engagement
- DMs received
- Demo calls booked

## Quick Reference: What Not to Do

- Do not use the word "chatbot" anywhere on your profile
- Do not leave sections blank
- Do not post generic real estate news. Only original perspective or original data
- Do not send connection requests without a note
- Do not pitch in the first message after connecting
- Do not use em dashes in any copy
- Do not say "passionate about" or "I am excited to" anywhere
- Do not connect with everyone. Stay focused on GCC agency decision-makers
